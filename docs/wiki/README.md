# AJENTIC Documentation

AJENTIC helps make AI-assisted work easier to inspect: look at the proposed
answer, see the recorded checks, and keep the review separate from a confident
claim that the work is finished. People remain responsible for what they trust.

**Start here:** the current browser experience is a local demonstration using
pre-made examples. You can practice reviewing a sample and recording a decision.
It is not a general-purpose AI chat app or a live connection to an AI service.

## What Will You Learn?

- **Users:** what AJENTIC helps you notice, how to review the sample, what the
  buttons mean, and when to stop and ask for help.
- **Developers:** how the repository fits together, where to make changes, how
  to test them, and how to contribute without changing who makes decisions.
- **Maintainers:** how to merge through CI without a second reviewer, handle
  failed checks, review dependency changes, and preserve release evidence.
